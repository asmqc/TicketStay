How it works

Ready in three steps

A few minutes at the start of the season, then TicketStay keeps track for you.

  1. 01Add your season ticketsPick your team from more than 460, then enter your sections, rows, seats and what the package cost.
  2. 02Import the scheduleAdd games from a CSV or XML file, or one at a time. Every seat gets its own status.
  3. 03Sell, give, get paidSuggested price, sales message, buyer book: you always know who has paid and who still owes you.
