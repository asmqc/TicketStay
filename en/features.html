Features

What the app does

Built for season ticket holders who resell or give away some of their tickets over the season.

A home screen that sums it all up

Your next game, what you're still owed and your current numbers, as soon as you open the app.

Schedule and tickets

Every game, every seat: for sale, sold, given away, used or to assign. Import your schedule from a CSV or XML file.

Buyer book

Who bought what, who still owes you, and a button to send a text reminder in one tap. Add someone by hand or in one tap from your contacts.

Suggested price

A price per ticket and per pair, based on your own sales and what your season tickets cost.

Listing mode

A screen that prepares the offer for each buyer, with an adjustable price, and tracks who has been contacted.

Messages drafted on device

Your device's built-in AI can draft a personalized sales message — nothing is sent anywhere.

PIN-protected account

A four-digit PIN protects your ticket management every time you open the app.

Multiple teams

As many season tickets as you like, in as many sports, with a favourite team that opens at launch.

Reminders and standings

A notification before a game whose tickets haven't been assigned, and live league standings.

Sharing and export

Share your season read-only or export your data to load it elsewhere, whenever you decide.

A clear status for every ticket
